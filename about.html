About PolyTrack Ranked

Race tracks, work on personal bests, and explore community additions built around Kodub's PolyTrack.

This is an unofficial community edition by StaticQuasar931. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Kodub, CrazyGames, Poki, or itch.io. PolyTrack was created by Kodub. For the original game, visit PolyTrack on CrazyGames.

Tracks and practice

Race official and community tracks, browse Extra Tracks, and keep local practice history and personal bests. Some tracks support replay viewing and ghost racing.

Timed events

Join daily and weekly community events. Each event shows its own schedule and scoring details; expired events do not reopen for scoring.

Ranked results

Eligible tracks can award Ranked Points. Verification status and missing results are labeled rather than assumed.

Personal filters

Customize which racers appear in your leaderboard view and save filter loadouts. Personal filters do not change public rankings or verification.
